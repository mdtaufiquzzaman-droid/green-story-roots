4️⃣ Business Description (Long Version – For About Page)

Botanica Living was created to connect people with plants in a deeper way.
We believe every plant has a story — a history rooted in culture, geography, and design.

Our platform allows customers to:

Discover carefully selected indoor plants

Learn about each plant’s origin and background

Understand detailed care instructions

Purchase with confidence

Soon access personalized botanical styling services

We combine education, design, and commerce into one seamless experience.
